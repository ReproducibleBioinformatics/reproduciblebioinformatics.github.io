Reproducible Bioinformatics
Data-driven, transparent and reusable computational biology

We build and promote reproducible workflows for bulk and single-cell/spatial omics, making computational analyses verifiable, portable and easy to reuse.

Our activities

Our group focuses on reproducible bioinformatics and single-cell / spatial omics. We work at the interface between wet lab and computational analysis, with a strong emphasis on transparency and long-term reusability.

  • Reproducible workflows for bulk RNA-seq, single-cell and spatial transcriptomics.
  • Containerised environments (Docker / Singularity) to freeze tools and dependencies.
  • FAIR and open science practices for sharing data, code and pipelines.
  • Training & teaching in reproducible analysis for students and collaborators.
